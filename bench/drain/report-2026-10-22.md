# bench drain: tofu session drain on a 20 MB session, 2026-10-22

## Headline

tofu session drain answers in a median of 805 ms on a 20.2 MB session of 11,298 events, 672 ms over the 132 ms floor of starting the binary at all. The slowest of 12 runs took 1,037 ms, so a caller waiting on drain needs more than one second of headroom, not 805 ms.

| arm | runs | p50 ms | min ms | max ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| drain, every run | 12 | 805 | 736 | 1,037 |
| drain, pass 1 | 6 | 791 | 736 | 811 |
| drain, pass 2 | 6 | 868 | 799 | 1,037 |
| floor, tofu version | 10 | 132.5 | 111 | 155 |

p95 over 12 runs is the slowest run, 1,037 ms.

## What the arms are

| arm | what runs | why it is there |
|---|---|---|
| cold | the first drain of a pass | the run a person sees when they first ask |
| warm | 5 drains after it, same session | the same read with the file in the page cache |
| floor | tofu version, 5 times | process start and exit with no session read, the cost drain cannot go under |

The floor is the baseline: whatever drain adds over it is the read and the sum, not the binary starting.

## Spread

| pass | cold ms | warm ms, in order | floor ms, in order |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 736 | 781, 766, 811, 794, 788 | 126, 111, 141, 132, 123 |
| 2 | 799 | 839, 1,037, 897, 836, 970 | 147, 155, 133, 139, 132 |

The cold run was not the slowest in either pass: pass 1 cold at 736 ms is the fastest of all 12. Pass 2 drain ran 77 ms slower at the median and its floor 13 ms slower (139 against 126); why is not known.

## Conditions

- Session: one real recorded session of 20,168,967 bytes and 11,298 event lines, timed by bench/drain/run.sh.
- Binary: the tree at b4cf8de6 with uncommitted changes, tofu 0.5.16-fix2 plus the drain verb; one binary for every row.
- Machine: DESKTOP-AHUN9RO, windows/amd64, Git Bash, wall clock from date +%s%N around each call, stdout to /dev/null.
- Two passes of run.sh on the same date, 22 rows: 12 drain, 10 floor. No run was discarded and no warm-up ran uncounted.
- No network, no model, no credential: drain reads the session file on disk.

## Rerun

    DRAIN_TOFU=<tofu binary> DRAIN_SESSION=<session id> bash bench/drain/run.sh
