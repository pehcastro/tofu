## Arm that won

**The readability rule, a free arm, and the judgment is not wired.** `catalog/policy/page_sift@1.yaml` still reads `mode: shadow`, so the model is given every fetched page whole. TOFU-236 ran this twice: the first round compared arms that had nothing to compare (both free arms removed almost nothing on 21 pages that had almost nothing to remove), so the corpus was widened to 24 with three reference pages carrying real link-list boilerplate, and chunking was added so a large page could be judged in more than one request. On the second round the free arm still won.
