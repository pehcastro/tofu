## What a row is

`at`, `session`, `action`, `event_id`, `event_kind`, `free_arm`, `chose`. Nothing else, and a test reads the raw file to prove it. `action` is `reached_into_work` or `moved_kind`. `event_kind` is the tool name, or `tool` for a settings move.

The free arm is read from the interface at the moment of the action, through `session.Model.FoldedOutOfChat`, never recomputed later. So a row says what the switch actually did at the time rather than what a rule written afterwards would have done.
