# bench/tools: three free search arms over 100 questions, and the corpus separates one of them

## Headline

**29 of 100** is the best a free arm does, `git grep` with one realistic term, and the corpus cannot tell it apart from `tofu search` at 18 of 100: 24 questions only the first gets, 13 only the second, exact two sided p = 0.10. The one separation the corpus does make is `bm25 alone` at 0 of 20, which is not close. `file_shortlist` gets no ranking from this file.

Cost unit: money. A money figure is dollars that left the account behind the credential named above, read from the response of the call it names and never from a rate card.

Every dollar figure in this report is a recorded figure from the run of 2026-09-21. **Nothing was spent today.** No model was called, no credential was read, and the three free arms need no network at all: they are `git grep` and an in-process BM25 rank, and they run on a machine with no upstream awake.

No call was discarded as a warm-up. The first call of every arm is in every number below.

## Conditions

Machine DESKTOP-AHUN9RO, Windows 10 Pro 19045, go1.27.1 windows/amd64, tree at 82f1a2e, 2026-09-22. `rg` is not installed on this machine. `git` is the one the repository uses.

Wall clock figures are a median and a worst per arm per slice, from the instrument. They are the cost of one search call and nothing else: no model, no turn, no reading of what came back.

**This is a tool table.** One call in, raw matches out. It is never set beside a vendor turn, which searches, reads and decides. The turn table lives in `bench/tools/report/turn_test.go` behind `TOFU_TOOLS_VENDOR=1` and did not run today.

## The corpus, before any score

**100 questions**, in `bench/tools/testdata/questions.jsonl`, counted off the file rather than read from a report.

```
trees      tofu 20, codex-main 20, cline 20, goose 20, gemini-cli 20
bands      named 20, described 40, intent 40
source     98 written by hand, 2 taken from recorded turns in .tofu/sessions
answers    98 with one right file and line, 2 broad audits scored on finding any one of a set
```

Where they came from: the `tofu` tree is this repository. The other four are clones under `.local/sources`, read only and not updated by this ticket. `jev-r2`, the 13,140 file size stress tree, is deliberately not one of the five: it is where a call is timed, not where a readable question is picked.

The bands are the whole design. A **named** question carries a backticked identifier that appears on its own answer line. A **described** question says what the code does in other words. An **intent** question asks why the code is the way it is. Named exists to be easy, and it is the control: an arm that cannot do named is broken.

## Leakage: checked, passes, nothing excluded

`bench/tools/corpus/discipline_test.go` fails any described or intent question whose content words match a path component, a file stem or a declared identifier of its own answer. It passes over all 100.

```
=== RUN   TestDescribedAndIntentQuestionsCarryNoAnswerVocabulary
--- PASS: TestDescribedAndIntentQuestionsCarryNoAnswerVocabulary (0.00s)
```

**Zero questions were excluded for leakage.** The exclusion happened when the corpus was built: the previous 30 question corpus had 25 questions naming a component of their own answer, three arms all scored 14 of 30 on it, and the tie measured the corpus rather than the tools. That corpus is gone and this one replaced it.

The 20 named questions leak by construction and are exempt from the test. That is what the band is for, and it is why the named row is read as a control rather than as a score.

- `tofu-04` answered `internal/turn/tools/grep.go:65`. **That file does not exist**: TOFU-284 deleted the grep tool on 2026-09-21. The question was unanswerable by every arm, and its miss measured the corpus. Replaced with a question over `internal/turn/tools/search.go:21`.

- `tofu-02` answered `bench/corpus/reader.go:195`. `WalkSessions` is at line 206 now. Repinned.
- `tofu-08` answered `bench/corpus/reader.go:155`, now a closing brace. Repinned to `ReadTurnDir` at 165.

All six carry a `note` in the corpus file saying what they were and why they moved.

**Each new line was chosen by reading the source and nothing else.** No arm output was consulted when picking it. That is the only defence against fitting a corpus to the arms scored on it, and it is a promise rather than a mechanism.

**The rot cost real accuracy and it was all on the free arm's side.** Before the repin, `git grep realistic` read 26 of 100 with 17 of 20 named. After, 29 of 100 with 20 of 20 named. Three of the corpus's own questions were scoring zero for every arm because the tree had moved, and a named question that no arm can get is a broken control.

`bm25 alone` ran only over the `tofu` tree, 20 questions: loading and BM25 ranking full file content for the other four was outside this run's time budget. Its `calls 0` is right, not a hole: it runs in process and shells out to nothing. It is graded as "is the answer's file the single top ranked file", the definition `bench/shortlist` already uses.

`tofu search` is graded more loosely than the other three: "does the answer's file path appear anywhere in the result", because `search.Find` returns whole declarations rather than line tagged matches. **Its 18 of 100 is over an easier bar than `git grep realistic`'s 29 of 100**, which makes the gap between them larger than it reads, not smaller.

## Separation: the corpus decides one comparison of four

```
    run_test.go:254: git grep realistic against tofu search over 100 all questions: 24 only the first, 13 only the second, 63 agree, exact two sided p = 0.10, not separable on this corpus
    run_test.go:254: git grep realistic against tofu search over 20 named questions: 16 only the first, 0 only the second, 4 agree, exact two sided p = 0.00, separated
    run_test.go:254: git grep realistic against tofu search over 40 described questions: 4 only the first, 9 only the second, 27 agree, exact two sided p = 0.27, not separable on this corpus
    run_test.go:254: git grep realistic against tofu search over 40 intent questions: 4 only the first, 4 only the second, 32 agree, exact two sided p = 1.00, not separable on this corpus
    run_test.go:254: git grep realistic against git grep quoted over 20 named questions: 0 only the first, 0 only the second, 20 agree, exact two sided p = 1.00, not separable on this corpus
```

This is a sign test over the paired per question outcomes, which the instrument now logs as a hit set per arm. A count of correct answers is not a comparison until the discordant pairs are counted, and 29 against 18 sounds decisive until you see that 63 of the 100 questions both arms answered the same way.

**Four readings, and three of them are a refusal.**

- Over all 100, `git grep realistic` beats `tofu search` at p = 0.10. **That is not separated and this report offers no number in its place.** It is a direction and a sample size, not a result.
- On named, `git grep realistic` wins 16 of the 16 questions they disagree on. **That is the control behaving**, not a finding: a question carrying the identifier that sits on its own answer line is exactly what grep is for.
- On described and on intent, the two arms are indistinguishable, and on intent they are a dead heat, 4 questions each way.
- `git grep realistic` and `git grep quoted` get the identical 20 named questions right. **Zero discordant pairs.** On this corpus a pattern built from every backticked term and a pattern built from the longest content word are the same arm.

`bm25 alone` is the one arm the corpus separates cleanly and it does not need a test: **0 of 20**, and the 20 questions it ran are 20 of the 100 the other arms scored 29 and 18 on. Ranking a whole file by term overlap against a question deliberately written to share no vocabulary with the code has nothing to work with. It is a real result and it kills the arm rather than measuring it.

## Skips, counted and named

**80 of 100, every described and every intent question**, skipped by `git grep quoted` for the missing field: no backticked term in the question text, so the arm has no pattern to build. The instrument names all 80 ids in its own output, `cline-05` through `cline-20`, `codexmain-05` through `codexmain-20`, `gemini-05` through `gemini-20`, `goose-05` through `goose-20`, `tofu-05` through `tofu-20`. That is the whole non-named corpus and it is by construction, not by accident. Its `20/100 overall` row is a scoring artefact: the arm answered 20 questions and got all 20 right.

`git grep realistic` and `tofu search` skipped nothing, 0 of 100 each. `bm25 alone` skipped nothing of the 20 it ran.

Test level skips in the default run, 4, all gated on an environment variable and all named in the output: `TestExtractRecordedTextSearchCalls` behind `TOFU_TOOLS_EXTRACT`, `TestTurnAgainstTurn` behind `TOFU_TOOLS_VENDOR`, `TestSearchOverTheLargestTreeOnThisMachine` behind `TOFU_TOOLS_LARGE_TREES`, and the five judged arms inside `TestToolAgainstTool` behind `TOFU_LIVE`.

## What it would take to separate the two general arms

At the ratio actually observed, 24 against 13 discordant, the corpus would need to roughly double. 200 questions at the same split gives 48 against 26 and p = 0.01; the present 100 gives p = 0.10. **That is 100 more questions written under the disqualifying rule**, which is the expensive part: the described and intent bands exist precisely because they are hard to write without naming the answer, and the leakage test rejects the easy ones.

**Do not read that as a recommendation to write them.** A bigger corpus of the same shape separates `git grep` from `tofu search` and answers nothing about `file_shortlist`, which is a ranking placement rather than a pattern placement, and whose free arm is BM25, which scored 0 of 20 here. The cheaper next measurement is the one that makes the judged rerun testable: record the per question hit set, which the instrument now does, and rerun the free arms and `jevgrep` in the same pass.
