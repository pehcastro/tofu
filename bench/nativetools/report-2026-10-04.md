## 1. edit against sed and a here-doc rewrite, 40 edits

Selection, spread evenly over recording order inside each kind: ambiguous 4 of 14, near-duplicate 12 of 13, block 12 of 175, one line 12 of 85, recorded edits valid at the seed.

Kinds: *ambiguous* means old_string occurs more than once, so the only right answer is no change and a reason. *near-duplicate* means one line changes and its text is held by more than one line of the file. *block* means the change is an insertion, a deletion or several lines. *one line* means one line changes and no other line holds its text. *wrong* means the file differs from the one the call asked for: on a rename, a line sed changed beyond the one asked for may be one the model meant to change next, and the report does not credit that.

Arms: *tofu edit* is `internal/turn/tools.Edit` called directly with the recorded arguments, its typecheck tail cut because the scratch copy has no tsconfig. *sed, changed line* is `sed -i 's/LINE/NEW/'` on the one line that changes, escaped in full, with no address, the way sed is used to save retyping the context; a block falls back to the next arm. *sed -z, whole old_string* is `sed -i -z 's/OLD/NEW/'` with the whole old_string, escaped in full. *here-doc rewrite* is `cat > path <<'EOF'` with the whole right file. Returned is what the arm prints back; sent is what the model writes to call it.
