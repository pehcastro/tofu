## What moved

`Render` no longer prints a mean over runs. It prints a median and a range per task per arm over repeats, and it refuses three comparisons it used to make.

Before, over the two recorded v2 rows, the frontier read:

```
boji vs claude: wall clock 235381ms vs 138715ms
```

Two single samples set side by side, which reads as a 70 percent gap. It now reads:

```
boji: wall clock 235381 ms on one repeat, which is a sample and not a spread
claude: wall clock 138715 ms on one repeat, which is a sample and not a spread
boji vs claude: wall clock not separable, boji and claude carry fewer than two passing repeats and one run has no spread
```

Four changes, all in `bench/harness`:

- a `Spread` holds the median, the low, the high and the repeat count, and every ranking line is printed from one
- a comparison where either arm has fewer than two passing repeats prints `not separable` instead of a winner
- a task whose door opens on one repeat and shuts on the next prints `UNSTABLE`, names the arm and the count, and ranks no arm at all
- a `ROWS READ` block heads every report, counting and naming rows dropped for a missing field

The recorded rows under `.playground/rows` and `bench/harness/testdata/v2-row/row.json` still render. `TestReportEveryArmFromTheRowsOnDisk` reads all four and prints both versions.

## What three repeats would cost

Read from the rows on disk, not guessed. Four runs are recorded and three of them carry a turn count and a wall clock:

| row | turns | wall clock | tokens in | tokens out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| claude hono v1 run1 | 14 | 63,457 ms | 393,063 | 11,025 |
| claude hono v2 run1 | 23 | 138,715 ms | 887,583 | 18,588 |
| boji hono v2 run1 | 35 | 235,381 ms | 631,362 | 17,924 |
| boji hono v1 run1 | none recorded | none recorded | none | none |

Median run: 23 turns, 138,715 ms, 631,362 tokens in, 17,924 out. Range: 14 to 35 turns, 63,457 to 235,381 ms.

Three arms, four tasks, three repeats each is **36 runs**.

- **Turns: 828 at the median**, 504 to 1,260 across the observed range.
- **Wall clock: 83 minutes of arm time at the median**, 38 minutes to 141 minutes across the range. Gate time is on top of this and has never been measured: `bun install`, `bun build`, `bun test`, `tsc` and the checker run once per run, 36 times.
- **Tokens: about 22.7 million in and 645 thousand out at the median.** Subscription quota on the claude and codex arms, so no dollar figure, and they are two different quotas that do not add.

Two caveats a person needs before spending it. Only two tasks exist, v1 and v2, so four tasks means writing two more prompts and two more checkers first; at the two that exist the figure halves to 18 runs and 42 minutes at the median. And the three measured runs come from two arms only: codex has no recorded row at all, because the one recorded `codex exec` transcript hit a usage limit and never reached `turn.completed`.

## The number this cannot produce

There is no rerun spread here, only an extrapolation from single runs, which is the very thing the change exists to stop reporting. The spread of a repeat against itself is unknown until repeats are actually run, and it stays unknown after this ticket.
